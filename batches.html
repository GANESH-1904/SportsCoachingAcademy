Holidays and exam season

Training should fit around school life, not fight it. Here is how timetables flex through the year.

  • Three terms a year. Fees and batches follow the school calendar.
  • Holiday camps. Multi-sport days run in school breaks, open to new and current athletes.
  • Exam-week light mode. Older athletes can drop to shorter sessions during board exams.
  • Weather cover. Outdoor batches move indoors or reschedule, with an instant notification.
