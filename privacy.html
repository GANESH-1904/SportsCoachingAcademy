Privacy Policy

How Kinetix Sports Academy collects, uses and protects your family’s information.

Last updated: October 2026. This is a starter policy; have it reviewed against your local legal requirements before launch.

What we collect

  • Parent details you give us: name, email and phone number.
  • Child details needed to place them safely: age, sport, batch and any health notes you choose to share.
  • Attendance, coach feedback and fee records created while your child trains.

How we use it

  • To enrol your child, schedule batches and contact you about sessions.
  • To share progress, attendance and fee receipts through the parent dashboard.
  • To keep every athlete safe, including emergency contact when needed.

Sharing and storage

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with staff who need it to coach and look after your child, and with payment providers to process fees.

Your choices

You can ask us to view, correct or delete your family’s information at any time at hello@kinetixacademy.example.
