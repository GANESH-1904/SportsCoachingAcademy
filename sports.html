Inside every session.

A typical training session

Whatever the sport, every session follows the same four-part plan, so children always know what comes next.

  1. Warm-up · 10 minDynamic mobility and movement games that prepare joints and muscles.
  2. Skill block · 25 minSmall-group technique work with one clear focus for the day.
  3. Game play · 20 minConditioned games and match scenarios that put the skill under pressure.
  4. Cool-down · 5 minStretching, hydration and a coach huddle on what went well.

Come prepared.

Kit guide for new joiners

You do not need to buy everything on day one. Here is what to bring for each group of sports.

Field

Football & cricket

  • Studs or turf shoes
  • Shin guards (football)
  • Whites and helmet (cricket)
  • Water bottle and cap
Court

Basketball, tennis & badminton

  • Non-marking court shoes
  • Racket (we lend to beginners)
  • Breathable sports tee
  • Wristband and towel
Pool

Swimming

  • Swimsuit or trunks
  • Cap and goggles
  • Towel and flip-flops
  • Change of clothes
Track

Athletics & conditioning

  • Running shoes
  • Shorts and light tee
  • Resistance band (optional)
  • Electrolyte drink
