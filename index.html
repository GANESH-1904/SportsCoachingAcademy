A day at Kinetix

Six days a week the academy hums from first light to 9 PM. Here is what a typical day looks like.

6:00 AM

Dawn track & pool

Athletics and swim squads start before school, when the air is cool.

4:30 PM

After-school football

Juniors arrive, warm up with ball games and split into skill groups.

6:00 PM

Court time

Basketball and badminton batches fill the indoor courts under the lights.

7:00 PM

Strength & cool-down

Mobility, core work and a guided stretch before the ride home.

A method that makes progress visible

  • Skill levels, not guesswork. Every child is assessed and placed in the right batch.
  • Small batches. Capped group sizes keep coach attention high.
  • Weekly feedback. Coaches log ratings and next steps after every cycle.
  • Safe by design. Certified first-aiders and age-appropriate load on every field.
