Started with one field.

Sport shapes character

Kinetix began in 2012 with a single football pitch and twenty children. Today more than 1,200 young athletes train with us across eight programmes.

We believe confidence, discipline and resilience are built in practice. Medals follow. Every session has a plan, every child has a goal and every parent can see progress.

Why we exist.

Mission and vision

Two commitments guide every decision we make, from the timetable to the team sheet.

Mission

Make quality coaching ordinary

Every child deserves a certified coach, a safe venue and a plan that fits their age. We design each session so beginners feel brave and experienced players feel stretched.

Vision

A city where every child plays

We want sport to be a lifelong habit for children across Chennai, whether that leads to a state podium, a school team or simply a healthier, happier adult life.
