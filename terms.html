Terms of Use

The simple rules for using the Kinetix website, dashboard and training programmes.

Last updated: October 2026. This is a starter document; have it reviewed before launch.

Enrolment and fees

  • Fees are charged monthly per batch. A one-time registration fee of ₹1,000 applies to monthly plans and is waived on annual plans.
  • Sibling discounts: 10% for a second child and 15% for a third.
  • You can cancel any month by telling us before the next billing date.

Attendance and make-ups

One make-up session per month can be booked from the dashboard, subject to space. Outdoor sessions may move indoors or be rescheduled in bad weather.

Safety

Children must follow coach instructions and wear suitable sportswear and footwear. Please tell us about any medical condition that could affect training.

Using the website

Keep your dashboard login private. Content on this site belongs to Kinetix Sports Academy and may not be copied without permission.
